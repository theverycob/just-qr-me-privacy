Privacy Policy

Just QR Me - QR Scanner & Generator
Effective July 18, 2026

QR scanning, current-link QR generation, printing, and History processing happen locally on your device. Just QR Me does not send scanned content, the current tab URL, decoded or generated QR values, History, or settings to the developer or to a developer-controlled server.

Information the extension handles

Just QR Me handles only the information needed to scan or generate QR codes and provide its user-facing features:

  • Current-tab content: visible page screenshots, page images, and image source URLs when you explicitly start a Page or Snip scan.
  • Current link: the active tab URL only when you explicitly choose Link.
  • User-provided images: images you choose, drop, or paste into the extension.
  • Scan and generated results: decoded QR values, a QR value generated from the current link, locally generated thumbnails, and timestamps.
  • Preferences: selected font, font size, popup width, bold setting, and high-contrast setting.

How information is used

The extension uses this information only to find and decode QR codes, generate a QR for the current link you select, copy results to your clipboard, open links you select, display recent History, rebuild and print a QR from a result you select, and remember your accessibility preferences.

Local processing and storage

QR decoding, current-link QR generation, and QR rebuilding occur inside the extension on your device. The Link action reads the active tab URL only after you choose Link and does not contact that website, the developer, or a third-party server to generate the QR. The generated result is stored in local History, which you can clear at any time.

Up to 50 recent decoded or generated results and their thumbnails are stored with chrome.storage.local. When you choose to print a History item, the selected value is temporarily passed to the extension's print page with chrome.storage.session. The extension does not use an account, cloud sync, analytics, telemetry, or a remote database.

When scanning an image tab or a full-resolution image already referenced by the current page, the extension may request that image directly from its existing source URL. The request goes to the website hosting that resource and is used only to complete the scan initiated by you. Scanned content and decoded results are not sent to the developer.

Sharing and selling

Just QR Me does not sell user data. The extension does not share user data with advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, or other third parties. User data is not used for advertising, creditworthiness, lending, or any purpose unrelated to the extension's QR features. Information voluntarily submitted through the separate support form is handled as described in the Contact section below.

Clipboard access

Decoded or locally generated QR content may be written to your clipboard when you choose Copy. The extension handles an image from the clipboard only when you intentionally paste that image into the extension.

Deleting your data

Select Clear in the History view to remove saved decoded and generated results. Removing the extension also removes its extension-local storage from Chrome.

External links

The extension can open a decoded or locally generated link at your request. It also includes an optional Buy Me a Coffee link. Once you open an external website, that website's privacy policy and practices apply. Just QR Me does not receive payment or financial information from Buy Me a Coffee.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Just QR Me's use of information obtained through Chrome permissions complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information is used only to provide or improve the extension's single-purpose, user-facing QR scanning, QR generation, History, and printing features.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices change, this policy and the Chrome Web Store disclosures will be updated before the new practices are introduced.
