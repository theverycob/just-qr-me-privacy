Privacy Policy

Just QR Me - A QR Scanner
Effective July 14, 2026

QR scanning and history processing happen locally on your device. Just QR Me does not send scanned content, decoded QR values, history, or settings to the developer or to a developer-controlled server.

Information the extension handles

Just QR Me handles only the information needed to scan QR codes and provide its user-facing features:

  • Current-tab content: visible page screenshots, page images, image source URLs, and the active tab URL when you explicitly start a Page or Snip scan.
  • User-provided images: images you choose, drop, or paste into the extension.
  • Scan results: decoded QR values, locally generated thumbnails, and scan timestamps.
  • Preferences: selected font, font size, popup width, bold setting, and high-contrast setting.

How information is used

The extension uses this information only to find and decode QR codes, copy results to your clipboard, open links you select, display recent scan history, and remember your accessibility preferences.

Local processing and storage

QR decoding occurs inside the extension on your device. Up to 50 recent scan results and their thumbnails are stored with chrome.storage.local. The extension does not use an account, cloud sync, analytics, telemetry, or a remote database.

When scanning an image tab or a full-resolution image already referenced by the current page, the extension may request that image directly from its existing source URL. The request goes to the website hosting that resource and is used only to complete the scan initiated by you. Scanned content and decoded results are not sent to the developer.

Sharing and selling

Just QR Me does not sell user data. It does not share user data with advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, or other third parties. User data is not used for advertising, creditworthiness, lending, or any purpose unrelated to QR scanning.

Clipboard access

Successful scan results may be written to your clipboard. The extension handles an image from the clipboard only when you intentionally paste that image into the extension.

Deleting your data

Select Clear in the History view to remove saved scan history. Removing the extension also removes its extension-local storage from Chrome.

External links

The extension can open a decoded link at your request. It also includes an optional Buy Me a Coffee link. Once you open an external website, that website's privacy policy and practices apply. Just QR Me does not receive payment or financial information from Buy Me a Coffee.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Just QR Me's use of information obtained through Chrome permissions complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information is used only to provide or improve the extension's single-purpose, user-facing QR scanning features.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices change, this policy and the Chrome Web Store disclosures will be updated before the new practices are introduced.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the public privacy repository's Issues page.
